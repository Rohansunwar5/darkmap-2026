Data-deletion instructions

Updated 12 September 2026

To request deletion of data associated with an Instagram or Facebook account connected to Darkmap:

  1. Remove Darkmap from the connected apps section of your Facebook or Instagram account settings to revoke future access.
  2. Send a deletion request to the contact address published for the Darkmap application in the Meta App Dashboard.
  3. Include the connected Instagram username and the words “Darkmap data deletion” in your request. Never send your password or access token.

After the account is verified, associated connection records and stored Instagram data will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized, except records that must be kept for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.
