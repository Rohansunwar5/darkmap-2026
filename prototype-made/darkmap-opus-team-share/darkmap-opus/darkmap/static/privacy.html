Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026

Darkmap is a brand-protection research service. It helps authorized users identify possible impersonation, counterfeit activity, fraud, phishing, and other brand abuse involving Instagram content.

Information we process

When an authorized user connects an Instagram source, Darkmap may process account and profile metadata, posts and reels, captions, hashtags, mentions, comments, public engagement metrics, media metadata, timestamps, and outbound URLs made available through that source. We also process search terms, brand reference data, risk-analysis results, and technical audit records.

How we use information

We use this information to perform searches requested by authorized users, normalize and index relevant records, detect potential brand abuse, create explainable risk scores, show supporting evidence, operate the service, enforce quotas, investigate errors, and protect the security of the service.

AI analysis

Relevant text and media metadata may be sent to an AI service to classify potential impersonation, scams, phishing, counterfeit activity, fake support behavior, suspicious links, and coordinated abuse. AI findings assist human review and should not be treated as final legal determinations.

Sharing and sale

Darkmap does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with infrastructure and analysis services needed to operate the service, when directed by an authorized user, or when required by law.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the brand-protection purpose, security, audit, and legal obligations. Access is restricted, credentials are kept out of application logs, and collection uses authorized interfaces with quotas, caching, retries, and audit records.

Your choices and deletion

You may disconnect Darkmap from Meta through your Facebook or Instagram account settings. You may also request deletion of data associated with your connected account by following the Darkmap data-deletion instructions.

Changes

We may update this policy as Darkmap changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to the contact address published for the Darkmap application in the Meta App Dashboard. Include the connected Instagram username and do not include an access token or password.
