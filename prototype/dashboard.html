How discovery works, and what it cannot promise
DarkMap pulls. Platforms do not push. No social platform offers a webhook for "someone just posted about your title." Results are live as of the moment you press scan, and nothing arrives between scans unless a monitor polls for it.

What each source mode means

ModeBehaviourFreshness you can claim
Live on searchThe page is fetched and rendered at scan time. Nothing is cached.Current to the second, for whatever the platform chooses to show.
Official APIQueried against the platform's own public API.Current, subject to the platform's publishing delay.
Search indexReads a search engine index rather than the live site.Up to several days behind. Backfill only, never the primary signal.

Three limits to state plainly

1. Coverage, not completeness. Hashtag pages filter and delay content the platform suspects breaches policy, which piracy content does. Some posts appear late, some never appear, private accounts never appear. No single source returns everything, which is why a scan fans out across several.

2. Index-backed sources lag. An account created four hours ago is not in any search engine's index and may never be.

3. Monitoring latency equals the polling interval. Once a title is on a watchlist, detection is as fast as that schedule and no faster.

How a score is built

Five components, fixed weights, computed the same way every time. In this prototype all five are deterministic. In production a model would contribute to title match and harm likelihood only, and never produce the total, so every score stays explainable.
